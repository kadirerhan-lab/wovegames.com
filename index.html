Our values

Child-safe by design

These are the threads that run through everything we make. If a feature pulls against them, it doesn't go in.

Child safety & privacy

Every game is designed for children first: safe content, privacy by default and parents kept in the loop. We collect as little as a game needs to run.

No ads, no manipulative purchases

No adverts. No loot boxes, no nagging timers, no "buy now or miss out". Anything that costs money is clearly marked and never aimed at a child.

Made with care

Stylized, hand-crafted low-poly worlds that stay bright and friendly — never realistic, never scary. We playtest with kids and families and listen closely.

Learning, woven in

Earning, saving, trying things and finding what you love. The lessons come from play, not from a lecture screen.

About the studio

A small studio with a big soft spot for kids

Wove Games is a founder-led independent game studio from Turkey, started by Kadir Erhan. We make games for children and the grown-ups who play alongside them.

The name comes from weaving dreams. Every child carries a handful of threads — curiosity, play, real-world skills and a little mischief — and we weave them into worlds kids want to return to. Small on purpose, careful by habit.

Build, try, earn, share, laugh. The best lessons are the ones you don't notice you're learning.
